The map is a schematic. The main parent schematic map has a region "play_area". For each team playing, a copy of this main schematic is made.
The map has a bunch of submaps, each of which are short little parkour obstacle courses that go up to the top. Each submap has a tag indicating difficulty of the particular level ("easy", "medium", "hard", "insane")
At the minimum corner of the play_area, the game randomly chooses a submap based on an algorithm. The difficulty of levels will always be in ascending order. Config will specify parameters of the algorithm that chooses the submap to place. The same submap will be placed in all copies of the original schematic.
Each team is teleported to the point "spawn" located within their specific copy of the parent schematic. They must enter a region "target" to be safe. Upon entering the target region, if the player tries to enter back into the region play_area they get teleported back into the region target.
Once the round starts, after a short delay lava will slowly start to rise 1 layer at a time. The speed at which the lava rises will increase over time, and the exact algorithm which determines lava speed will have its parameters tweakable in the config. If a player dies in any way, they become a spectator (and the only spectatable players they will be allowed to spectate are their own teammates- an API change might be needed so spectatable players/teams are per player and not a global list)
If there are no non-spectating players on any team who have not reached the target and completed the room, the room ends. Then, after a short delay, players are teleported back to the point spawn, the play_area is cleared, and a new submap is placed in the same spot according to the algorithm and the process repeats. The rate of the lava rising will be the same for all teams and copies of the map. The lava's x and z dimensions will be constrained to the play area, and it is the y dimension that rises until it fills up the entire play_area region.
When a new room starts, if all players on a team who were non-spectators when the round starts successfully complete the room without dying, then any spectating players get revived for the next round/room. (So say for example, there is a team with players A, B, C, and D. In the first room, A dies and starts spectating but B, C, and D all survive. In the second room, C dies and starts spectating, so now A and C are spectators and B and D are alive. Then in the third room, both B and D complete it without dying. When the fourth room starts, A and C are revived as well at the point spawn and now all four players A, B, C, and D are alive again.)
When all players on a team are spectating, the team is eliminated. When a team gets eliminated, the game checks how many and which teams are alive. When there is one team alive, the game ends and that team is the winner.
